# Pharmacovigilance Internship – Task 5

## Individual Case Safety Report (ICSR)

### Objective

To perform a complete pharmacovigilance assessment of an Individual Case Safety Report (ICSR), including ADR identification, case validity assessment, seriousness assessment, expectedness assessment, causality assessment, case classification, and narrative preparation.

### Task Overview

This task simulates the role of a Drug Safety Associate in processing and assessing an individual safety case.

### Key Deliverables

- ICSR validity assessment
- Patient and case information review
- Suspected and concomitant medication assessment
- Adverse reaction identification
- Seriousness assessment
- Expectedness assessment
- Causality assessment
- Dechallenge and rechallenge assessment
- MedDRA coding considerations
- ICSR narrative
- Case classification
- Quality review considerations

### ICSR Validity

A valid ICSR requires the following four minimum criteria:

1. An identifiable patient
2. An identifiable reporter
3. At least one suspected medicinal product
4. At least one suspected adverse reaction

If these minimum criteria are available, the case can be considered a valid ICSR for further processing.

### ADR Identification

The reported adverse event(s) were reviewed in relation to the suspected medicinal product, patient information, and available timeline.

The assessment considered the temporal relationship between drug exposure and the onset of the reported reaction.

### Seriousness Assessment

The case was assessed using standard pharmacovigilance seriousness criteria, including outcomes such as hospitalization, life-threatening events, death, disability, congenital anomaly, and other medically important conditions where applicable.

### Expectedness Assessment

Expectedness should be assessed against the applicable current Reference Safety Information (RSI) or relevant product safety information.

The expectedness assessment in this educational exercise should therefore be verified against the applicable product information before being used in a real pharmacovigilance process.

### Causality Assessment

Causality was assessed by considering:

- Temporal relationship
- Dechallenge information
- Rechallenge information
- Alternative causes
- Concomitant medications
- Available clinical information

The assessment should distinguish between reported information and the assessor's interpretation.

### MedDRA Coding

MedDRA terminology should be used for coding reported adverse events and relevant medical information.

The appropriate MedDRA Lowest Level Term (LLT) and Preferred Term (PT) should be selected according to the current licensed MedDRA version and the reported information.
### ICSR Narrative

The narrative presents the relevant case information in chronological order, including:

- Patient information
- Suspected medication
- Indication
- Dose and route
- Adverse reaction
- Relevant dates
- Investigations
- Treatment or action taken
- Outcome
- Seriousness
- Relevant medical history
- Concomitant medication

The narrative should remain objective and should not introduce information that was not reported.

### Quality Review Considerations

The case should be checked for:

- Minimum ICSR criteria
- Completeness of patient and reporter information
- Correct suspect drug information
- Correct adverse event information
- Accurate chronology
- Seriousness classification
- Expectedness assessment
- Appropriate MedDRA coding
- Duplicate case considerations
- Narrative accuracy
- Follow-up requirements

### Conclusion

This task demonstrates the overall workflow involved in Individual Case Safety Report processing and pharmacovigilance case assessment. It integrates ADR identification, ICSR validity assessment, seriousness and expectedness assessment, causality evaluation, MedDRA coding considerations, case classification, and narrative writing.

### Key Pharmacovigilance Learning Points

- Always verify the minimum criteria required for a valid ICSR.
- Maintain an accurate chronology of the case.
- Distinguish seriousness from severity.
- Assess expectedness against the applicable Reference Safety Information.
- Consider dechallenge, rechallenge, and alternative causes during causality assessment.
- Use MedDRA terminology appropriately.
- Keep ICSR narratives factual, concise, chronological, and objective.
- Do not add or assume information that has not been reported.
- Perform appropriate quality checks before case submission.

### Disclaimer

This is a simulated educational case prepared for Pharmacovigilance Internship training purposes. It is not a real patient safety report and should not be used for actual regulatory or clinical decision-making. Expectedness and MedDRA coding should be verified against the applicable product information and current licensed MedDRA version in a real pharmacovigilance environment.
